## 2. Research Focus & Goals
1. Execute a full-length structured integrative review synthesizing popular-music studies, cultural geography, developmental psychology, communication, public health, and peace/conflict studies.
2. Interrogate deterministic stereotypes regarding music genres (rock, metal, hip-hop, pop, folk-fusion) and social conduct.
3. Anchor cross-cultural analysis with Bangladesh as the primary case study (Linguistic nationalism, Liberation War broadcasting, Rabindrasangit, Dhaka metal scene, hip-hop activism).
4. Outline an empirical multisite, longitudinal mixed-method study design.
